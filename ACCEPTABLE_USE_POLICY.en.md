The following is prohibited:
1. Conducting, facilitating, or supporting activities that violate applicable laws or regulations.
2. Accessing, testing, modifying, or disrupting systems, accounts, data, or infrastructure without lawful authorization.
3. Distributing malware, committing fraud, phishing, spamming, or engaging in conduct that endangers users or systems.
4. Collecting, disclosing, manipulating, or misusing personal or third-party data without a lawful basis and authorization.
5. Impersonating TCS, its administrator, a Participant, or another party, or claiming unauthorized affiliation or authority.
6. Infringing copyrights, licenses, trademarks, or other intellectual property rights.
7. Creating or using duplicate accounts on TCS services enforcing a one-account-per-individual policy.
8. Using Choco AI outside the TCS ecosystem or repurposing it as a general-purpose chatbot.
9. Operating a separate system within TCS membership that conflicts with TCS systems and governance where integration is required.
