# The Choco Studios (TCS) Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 6 November 2026  
**Privacy contact:** thechocostudio@gmail.com

The Choco Studios (TCS) respects the privacy of users and Participants in its ecosystem. Data processed, purposes, and retention periods may vary by feature, architecture, and service. Each service that collects data must describe its actual practices in a relevant notice.

## 1. Data That May Be Processed
Depending on the service, data may include information directly provided, account or identity information, technical data, usage records, and security logs. TCS does not claim that every category is collected by every service.

## 2. Processing Purposes
Data may be used proportionately to provide and operate services, manage accounts or membership, provide support, maintain security, improve quality, comply with legal obligations, and enforce TCS Terms and rules.

## 3. Storage and Third Parties
Some applications may store data locally; other services may use hosting, databases, or third-party technology providers. Storage and retention depend on each service’s architecture and notice. Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies.

## 4. Disclosure and Sale
TCS does not sell users’ personal data. Data may be disclosed when necessary to operate services, comply with legal obligations or lawful requests, or protect the security and rights of TCS, users, or others, subject to law.

## 5. Security
TCS applies reasonable safeguards according to its capabilities and the nature of the service. No electronic system can be guaranteed entirely risk-free.

## 6. User Rights
Requests concerning access, correction, deletion, or other personal data rights may be submitted to TCS’s privacy contact. Requests are handled in accordance with law, technical capabilities, and lawful retention obligations.

## 7. Cookies
Certain sites or services may use cookies or similar technologies for technical functions, security, or preferences. Actual practices may vary by service.

## 8. Children and Sensitive Data
TCS services must not be used to process data unlawfully, including children’s or sensitive data without the required legal basis and safeguards.

## 9. Language and Updates
Indonesian is the primary language. Translations may be provided for international use. If a translation differs, the Indonesian version prevails unless otherwise required by law. TCS may publish revisions and their effective dates.

## 10. Contact
Privacy questions or requests may be sent to **thechocostudio@gmail.com**. This policy is general; services with specific processing practices must provide a relevant privacy notice.
